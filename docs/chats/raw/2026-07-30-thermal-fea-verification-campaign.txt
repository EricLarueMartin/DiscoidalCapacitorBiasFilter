Key results:

- Zero delta_T raw maximum: 0 MPa.
- Homogeneous free-contraction raw maximum: 1.2137e-11 MPa.
- Fully restrained result: 37.00000000000005 MPa versus 37 MPa closed form.
- Temperature ratio: exactly 2.0 for 50 C versus 25 C drops.
- Common modulus ratios: exactly 0.5 and 2.0.
- Common CTE offset relative change: 2.59e-13.
- Mirror/full P99 difference: 0.00472%; P95 difference: 0.509%.
- 0.15 to 0.10 mm mesh change: P95 0.071%, P99 0.883%, raw maximum 16.5%.
- Raw corner peak rises from 40.612 MPa at 0.60 mm to 78.884 MPa at
  0.10 mm and is not converged.

Interpretation:

The current linear-thermoelastic implementation and exact symmetry reduction
are verified for comparative analysis. The campaign does not validate the
assumed MG 9510 modulus, perfect adhesion, the 70 C stress-free reference,
cure shrinkage, viscoelasticity, cohesive fracture, or a separation
temperature.
