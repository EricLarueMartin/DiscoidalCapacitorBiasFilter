User: CV/e for 5 mV and 2 pF is about 62,500 electrons. Where does approximately
65 dB of 50 Hz noise attenuation come from? Wants a breakdown to reconcile it.

Assistant reran exact intermediate-node 50 Hz simulations and an independent
2 pF charge transient. The 65 dB comparison mixes 2 pF peak charge with the
previous 10 pF RMS result. Consistent model totals are 73.35 dB at 2 pF and
75.67 dB at 10 pF (9.49 and 36.32 electrons RMS). Approximately 21 dB comes
from the physical filter/cables, 19 dB from the slow sinusoidal preamplifier
response relative to a fast charge pulse, and 33-36 dB net from the trapezoid
after accounting for its attenuation of the signal calibration. Pole-zero is
off for this comparison. Assumptions and full arithmetic are documented in
docs/knowledge/supply-50hz-breakdown.md.
