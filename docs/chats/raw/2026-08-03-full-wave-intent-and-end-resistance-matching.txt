2026-08-03: Full-wave intent and end-resistance matching

The intended high-frequency structural analysis is a damped RF-cavity transfer
calculation that includes magnetic energy, cavity/antenna modes, radiation,
and material loss. The existing axisymmetric electroquasistatic FEniCSx sweep
is retained as a distributed-RC validation limit and must not be described as
the full-wave result. Its slide was renamed accordingly.

The planned full-wave FEniCSx model uses the Maxwell curl-curl equation,
free-space defaults for unspecified regions, explicit material overrides,
radiation boundaries or PML around open space, and Poynting-flux accounting.
An axisymmetric solve is the first reference; 3D is required for asymmetric
ports, leads, cavity modes, and the MELF film geometry.

Input/output resistance matching now follows the stage definition. A directly
entered discrete stage resistance is reused exactly at a checked end. A bulk
resistive material instead retains the same conductivity and cross-sectional
area, with resistance calculated from the end region's own length. In the
current symmetric geometry:

Lstage = ground plate thickness + 2 dielectric gaps
Lend = ground plate thickness + 1 dielectric gap
Rend / Rstage = Lend / Lstage

For a discrete resistance represented in a field model, the homogeneous
surrogate conductivity is sigma = L/(R A). A selected bulk material retains
one common conductivity and allows resistance to vary with L/A.

The direct capacitance override follows the same surrogate idea but remains
independent of the resistance choice. The entered Cpar is the resistor package
alone, before installation. Field models fit the core-region permittivity as

epsr_eff = Cpar_package Lstage / (epsilon0 Acore)

so that the ideal cylindrical core reproduces the entered terminal
capacitance. Epoxy outside the core is still meshed with the selected epoxy
permittivity and contributes a separate parallel coupling path. Including
that external path in the entered package Cpar would double count it.

The first full-wave implementation now uses MEEP 1.29 in cylindrical m=0
coordinates rather than relabeling the FEniCSx scalar EQS model. Undefined
space is vacuum and radial/axial PML layers absorb emitted fields. Radial
lumped ports span the core-to-ground apertures at the first and final ground
plates. The input, interstage, and output core pieces each use sigma=L/(RA)
with their own specified resistance; the saved default is 12 Mohm, 12 Mohm,
and 50 ohm respectively.

The convergence-audited 3-cells/mm default sweep covers 100 MHz to 30 GHz.
All six points met a 1e-4 CW residual target. Transfer is about -99.19 dB at
100 MHz, rises to -11.88 dB at 10 GHz, and returns to -57.80 dB at 30 GHz. The
10 GHz feature requires a denser frequency sweep and mesh comparison.

The first radiation-monitor implementation used unshifted z coordinates after
centering the MEEP cell. Its upper monitor was outside the cell, producing an
impossible exact-zero output flux and invalid signed percentages. Corrected
centered-cell monitors give nonzero output flux. Raw signed Poynting flux is
retained; the slide normalizes only positive outward power.
